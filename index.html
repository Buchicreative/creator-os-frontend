FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan gives you 5 idea generations and 3 content pieces per day, script and caption output, video resizing up to 3 minutes, and up to 10 saved projects. No credit card required.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Absolutely. You can cancel from your Settings panel at any time. You'll keep full access until the end of your current billing period, then automatically move to the Free plan.

Does it work for any niche?

Yes. Crevers supports 23 niches out of the box: from fitness and finance to fashion, real estate, music, gaming, and more. You can also type in a custom niche if yours isn't listed.

Is my content saved?

Yes. Every piece of content you generate can be saved as a project. Free users can save up to 10 projects; Pro and Creator users get unlimited storage.

What video formats does the resizer support?

The video resizer accepts MP4, MOV, and WebM files. Output is in MP4 format (or WebM as fallback). Supported aspect ratios: 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), 1:1 (Instagram), 4:5 (Feed), and 16:9 (YouTube).

Is my data private?

Yes. Your content, projects, and account information are stored securely and never shared with third parties. Content you generate belongs to you.

Does Crevers have a voiceover generator?

Yes. The Voiceover Generator lets you paste any script, pick from 6 AI voices, adjust the speed, and download a professional MP3 instantly. It is available on Pro and Creator plans. Pro users get 5 voiceovers per day with a 2000 character limit. Creator users get unlimited voiceovers with a 4000 character limit.

Does Crevers have a content calendar?

Yes. The built-in Content Calendar lets you plan your week by day and platform, add content ideas, mark posts as done, track your posting streak, and generate content directly from a calendar entry. Free for all users.

Is there an AI chat-bot on Crevers?

Yes. Crevers includes an AI Chat-bot built directly into the dashboard. Ask it anything about content strategy, growth, hooks, or trends. Available 24/7 and free for all users.

What platforms can I create content for?

Crevers supports Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Facebook, and WhatsApp. You can also enter a custom platform. The AI adapts the tone, length, and format to match each platform.
