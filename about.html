The problem we are actually solving

Almost everyone building something knows they should be showing up online. Very few manage it consistently. The usual explanation is discipline or time, and we think both are wrong.

The real cost is that every time you sit down to post, you start from nothing. What do I post about, which platform, what format, how do I open it, is this any good. That is five decisions before a single word gets written. Multiply it across three posts a week and the decisions drain you before the work begins.

So Monday you have energy and you post. Wednesday you are tired and you skip it. By Friday you have decided to start again properly next month.

Crevers is built for that specific moment. Not to replace your judgement, but so you never sit down to nothing.

Who it is for

When people hear content tool they think influencers. Most of our users are not.

  • Business owners who know they should be posting and are not
  • The person at a company who somehow ended up running social media
  • Coaches, consultants and freelancers building an audience alongside the actual work
  • Restaurants, shops and local services competing for attention online
  • Creators who post often enough that the blank page is a daily tax

You do not need to be a creator. You need something worth saying and not enough time to say it.

What we will not do

Crevers does not post for you, and it is not meant to flood the internet with generated filler. It gives you the draft so your energy goes into making it sound like you, rather than into staring at nothing.

The strategy stays yours. The voice stays yours. The judgement about what is true and worth saying stays yours. We are the outline, not the author.

Where we are built

Crevers is built in Port Harcourt, Nigeria, for creators everywhere.

That matters more than it might sound. Building from here means we think hard about people on mobile data, on mid-range phones, on connections that drop. It is why there is a free plan that is genuinely free, and why the paid plans cost what they cost.
